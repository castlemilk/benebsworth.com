# Compose style, theme and topic

Choose these independently before generating an image:

- Style: material, photographic or drawn treatment, lighting, crop, degree of abstraction.
- Theme: editorial desk and mood, such as personal field notes, engineering, science or an object study.
- Topic: one concrete metaphor for the article's particular argument.

The approved personal essay reference uses paper, ink, a restrained orange shape and a pencil comparison. Reuse its restraint, not the same circle for every article. An engineering article may use a measured technical study; a science article may use a physically coherent specimen or apparatus. Avoid generic AI blobs and decorative category colours.

Write a short brief combining all three inputs, plus output dimensions, crop-safe area and exclusions. Check the result at thumbnail size. Keep evidence screenshots separate from generated art and caption their provenance. Do not label an article human-written merely because its image has a hand-drawn style. Preserve original screenshot meaning and disclose any crop or redaction.
