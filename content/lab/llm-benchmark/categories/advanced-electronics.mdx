## Advanced Electronics

This category asks for an interactive circuit solver. The model must understand the second-order dynamics of a series RLC circuit, classify the damping regime, and implement a numeric solver that stays stable as the user sweeps $R$, $L$, and $C$.

The artifact must plot the step response live and give immediate visual feedback when the circuit moves from underdamped ringing to overdamped sluggishness.

### Common failure modes

- **Wrong damping classification** — treating every response as underdamped regardless of $\alpha$ vs $\omega_0$.
- **Unstable explicit integration** when the time step is too coarse for the chosen $L$ and $C$.
- **Missing units or mislabelled axes** make the plot unreadable.

### Task: Circuit Builder Teaser

Build a Canvas 2D page that adjusts $R$, $L$, $C$ and plots the series RLC step response. Correctness of damping classification and solver stability drive the score.
