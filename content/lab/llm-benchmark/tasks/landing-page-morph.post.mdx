## What the results mean

UI generation is part layout, part taste. Claude 4 scored highest by producing accessible landmarks, consistent spacing, and a theme toggle that actually flipped CSS variables. GPT-5 was fast and structurally sound but leaned on default browser styles. Gemini 2.5 Pro generated the most ambitious motion design, though one animation broke at narrow viewports.

Cost here is dominated by the long HTML/CSS output. Iterations matter: the second or third sample often cleans up the responsive breakpoints the first pass missed.
