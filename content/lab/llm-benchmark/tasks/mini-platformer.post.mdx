## What the results mean

Playability beats polish here. GPT-5 shipped the smallest, most runnable Canvas loop and scored well on collision stability. Claude 4 added more features (parallax, particles, reset UI) but introduced a subtle bug where the player could wall-jump indefinitely. Gemini 2.5 Pro generated clean React-style structure but sometimes over-abstracted a single-file task into multiple pseudo-modules.

The takeaway: for small interactive games, the model that respects the "single file" constraint and tests edge cases wins.
