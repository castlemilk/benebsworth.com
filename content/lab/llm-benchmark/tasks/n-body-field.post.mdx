## What the results mean

The n-body task rewards **correct integration** over sheer token volume. Claude 4 produced the most stable softening kernel and kept the particle cloud from collapsing into hot clumps, while GPT-5 was faster but used a simpler Euler step that drifted after a few thousand frames. Gemini 2.5 Pro sat in the middle: good physical intuition, slightly heavier shaders.

The cost spread is worth watching. Long outputs (full Three.js scenes) push output-token pricing hard, so a model that answers tersely but correctly can win on dollars even if it loses on wall-clock time.
