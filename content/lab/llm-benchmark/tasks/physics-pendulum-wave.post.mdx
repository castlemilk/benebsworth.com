## What the results mean

A pendulum wave is a **period-matching** problem disguised as animation. The model has to derive slightly different lengths so the phases realign at a beat frequency. Claude 4 got the period relation right and exposed clean sliders for N and gravity. GPT-5 produced a pretty loop but hard-coded phase offsets rather than physical lengths.

This task separates models that simulate physics from models that animate approximations. The difference shows up the moment you change the gravity slider.
