## Carry a direction around a triangle

The route follows two meridians and an equatorial arc. The amber arrow is parallel transported, while the blue arrow remains at the north pole as a reference. Scrub time through the full route and compare their final directions.

## Try these checks

1. Choose **One octant**. The three interior angles are each 90°, and the final arrow is 90° from its starting direction.
2. Choose **Narrow triangle**. A 30° longitude gap gives a 30° holonomy magnitude.
3. Choose **Larger sphere**. The enclosed area rises and Gaussian curvature falls. Their product, the angle in radians, stays the same.

The readouts describe the completed loop. At intermediate times the arrow is still travelling. The animation uses exact rotations along great-circle arcs, which preserve tangency and arrow length.

This vector is not a magnetic compass. The model concerns the geometry of tangent directions on a round sphere. The radius has arbitrary length units, so the area and curvature use the corresponding squared and inverse-squared units.
