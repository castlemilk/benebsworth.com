## Follow the field tip

The long axis is the direction of propagation. The cyan curve samples the electric field at many positions at one instant. The violet ellipse records the field tip's path over a cycle at one fixed position. The amber axis is an ideal linear analyser.

1. Select **Circular**. Rotate the camera to look along the beam, then scrub the timeline. The field tip keeps a constant radius.
2. Change **Phase delay** to 0°. The same component amplitudes now make a line.
3. Select **Linear** and set **Analyser angle** to 135°. Mean transmission falls to zero.
4. Select **Circular** again. Every analyser angle transmits 50% of the mean intensity.
5. Try **Elliptical**: the default 30° analyser transmits 68.1%, and the minor-to-major axis ratio is 0.400.

The incident time-averaged squared field is fixed at 0.5 in normalised units. The analyser readout measures an average; the arrow shows an instantaneous projection. The helix is a field diagram, not a photon trajectory.
