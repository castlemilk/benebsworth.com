## Start with a prediction

The blue unit sphere contains equal-length input vectors. The green ellipsoid is their image under a linear map. Amber markers identify one corresponding input and output as you scrub time.

Choose **Volume preserved**. Predict whether changing **Output rotation** should alter the condition number, then move the control. Orthogonal output rotations change directions but preserve lengths, volume factor, and rank.

## Three experiments

1. Choose **Almost singular**. The minimum stretch is 0.1, so an output error in that direction is multiplied by 10 during inversion.
2. Choose **Flatten to a plane**. Rank drops to 2, and the condition number becomes infinite. An entire input direction has disappeared.
3. Return to **Volume preserved** and compare the three axis lengths. Their product is 1 even though the condition number is 4.

The controls construct a matrix from known singular factors. They do not numerically decompose an arbitrary input matrix, and the stretch controls are axis-labelled rather than sorted. The original input rotation is fixed at 30°.
