## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal folio with three distinct reading rhythms, not a repeated grid of spotlight cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper or existing dark graphite, generous serif titles, quiet structural rules, condensed labels and restrained red handwriting. Existing portrait, trail art and project emblems carry identity; no generated biography, photographs or metrics.

STORY: Meet Ben through his portrait and career, explore completed and planned trails, then open the actual projects. About retains its interactive portrait, skill provenance and recordings.

FIRST VIEWPORT: About pairs a large name and real biography with the interactive portrait. Hiking leads into a wide Alta Via 1 trail plate and caption. Projects leads into a ruled Nutry entry with its original emblem. Small screens preserve this reading order and move annotations above their targets.

FORM: Extension of the owner-selected research folio, informed by the generated three-page concept board. The board is inspiration, not an approved pixel-perfect comp or source of text/images. No new-world seed applies. The optional Impeccable context engine could not run because its cache is unavailable; context and craft checks are manual.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
