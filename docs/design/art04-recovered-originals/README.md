## Independent review

The six originals were visually inspected on 17 November 2026. Engraved graphite/ivory material and restrained red details fit the exploratory folio direction; that is a subjective concept assessment, not acceptance of a rendered reading surface. The circuit is landscape; the other five are square. Subjects have tight margins in several images, so contain rather than crop them. The black-hole shadow can merge into a dark surface.

Alpha inspection confirms transparent and partial-alpha pixels, but these are not flat binary cutouts: nearly all nontransparent pixels are partially transparent. Edge colour/fringe appearance and contrast still require actual light/dark browser review at intended sizes. Do not inherit the source session's no-halo conclusion as independently verified. Keep approved production symbols until consumer review is complete.
