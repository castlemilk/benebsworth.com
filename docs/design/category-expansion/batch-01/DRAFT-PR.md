# Add science and living systems category artwork studies

## Why

The blog needs subject-specific artwork for the four existing science topics and six proposed science/living-system category studies. This batch extends the approved graphite, ivory and restrained-red research folio while keeping taxonomy and production symbols unchanged.

## Changes

- Ten separately generated category concepts, with eleven original PNGs retained because the first neuron framing is preserved beside its corrected revision.
- Exact generation requests, settings available to the tool, SHA-256 manifests, source/upload receipts and incremental recovery evidence.
- A standalone local gallery, light/dark contacts, 256/128/64 px comparisons, 1:1 edge samples and an independent image review.
- Accessible usage notes, explicit existing/proposed taxonomy labels and scientific-metaphor boundaries.

Only `docs/design/category-expansion/batch-01/` and `public/artwork/category-expansion-v1/batch-01/` change. ART04, custom-v6, official technology marks, content, registries and all concurrent owner files remain intact.

## Validation

Asset integrity, dimensions, alpha and exact generated/uploaded byte hashes are checked by `verify-assets.py`. Independent raster review found no broad visible halos or clipped substantial tips in the selected family. Neuron revision 2 fixes the crowded upper margin. Other measured margins and faint alpha 1/255 border residues are documented.

No application runtime was changed. Full application CI and real-browser acceptance are not claimed. The gallery's browser layout and any future consuming-route adoption still require review.
