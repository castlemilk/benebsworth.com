# Batch 01 artwork usage

These raster studies explore ten science and living-systems category metaphors within the Blog/Lab folio family. They are review assets pending owner selection and consuming-page review. Keep current topic symbols and registry mappings until that separate integration is approved.

## Meaning and labels

Use the existing labels Mathematics, Chemistry, Life Sciences and Earth & Space when referring to their verified topic keys. Geology, Oceanography, Meteorology, Ecology, Genetics and Neuroscience must be visibly identified as proposed category studies wherever an interface could otherwise imply that they are established categories.

An artwork file does not establish an article, source, dataset, research result or relationship in the knowledge graph. Do not manufacture a category page, filter entry or content association to accommodate an image.

| Study | Interpretation boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Mathematics | The carved folded surface is an abstract geometry metaphor; it does not encode a named equation or verified mathematical construction. |
| Chemistry | The crystalline molecular assembly is a symbolic material study, without a claimed molecule, bond structure or crystallographic result. |
| Life Sciences | The fern and seed form an intentional assemblage of living-system motifs. It is not a model of fern reproduction and does not imply that ferns produce seeds. |
| Earth & Space | The cutaway terrestrial globe is a category metaphor, not a measured interior model or a source of layer depths, proportions or geographic data. |
| Geology | The layered rock is an invented specimen study, not a documented sample, locality, geological date or stratigraphic record. |
| Oceanography | The wave cross-section is expressive artwork, not a computed fluid field or a measured ocean event. |
| Meteorology | The cloud and barometric instrument are a metaphor; the instrument supplies no calibrated reading or weather observation. |

## Accessible treatment

When an artwork sits beside a visible category label and adds no additional information, render it as decorative with `alt=""`. The visible label and surrounding text carry the category's accessible name and its proposed status. If the artwork is inside a link with visible text, avoid repeating that text in the image alternative.

An isolated image whose subject matters to the reader needs a concise descriptive alternative. Match the actual selected image rather than relying on its filename. Examples for this study family include:

- “Graphite engraving of a carved folded surface with a small red detail.”
- “Fern frond and seed shown together as a symbolic life-sciences assemblage.”
- “Stylized branching neuron rendered in graphite and ivory.”

Do not hide “proposed category study” exclusively in an image alternative: sighted readers need the same status in ordinary visible text. Artwork is never the sole source of a control's name, focus state, selection, error or loading information. Do not present red details as an operational status signal.

For a download/gallery figure, use a visible caption naming the concept and its study status. Keep long provenance and scientific limitations in selectable adjacent text. A thumbnail can have an empty alternative when its linked caption already identifies the same destination.

## Size, crop and clear space

Preserve the complete subject with contain-style placement and natural aspect ratio. Do not use a fill crop for an avatar, circular badge or uniform tile without separate subject review. Give the image deliberate whitespace within the surrounding folio; internal transparent pixels are not a substitute for layout spacing.

The generation prompts request approximately 16% clear margins. That is a requested composition target, not a measured guarantee. Inspection of the initial mathematics and chemistry pair found low-alpha residue near raw canvas edges and did not establish the requested margin. Preserve that finding in the review record; do not describe either original as a clean 16%-margin cutout. The final per-file alpha bounds and edge observations remain authoritative for all ten files.

For an initial consumer study, use approximately 160–320 CSS-pixel illustration boxes in category/header compositions and inspect each actual subject at its intended size. Larger plates may retain more engraved texture; small markers can lose texture and fine connections. Review 32, 48 and 96-pixel presentations before proposing thumbnail use, and retain current approved symbols where the new subject is no longer legible. These sizes are review suggestions, not accepted minimums.

Keep titles, captions, annotation text and any red reading connector outside the raster. A connector must point to a meaningful feature with real clearance. Do not add arbitrary labels, scientific values, sequential numbering or detached pointers to make the illustration appear authoritative.

## Transparency and light and dark surfaces

PNG format and an RGBA mode alone do not prove useful transparency. Consult the manifest and alpha inspection for transparent, partial-alpha and opaque pixel counts, bounding boxes and margins. Sparse low-alpha edge residue can remain even where a reduced preview appears visually clear.

Review unmodified originals against the actual warm-ivory reading surface and dark reading surface at intended sizes. Check graphite shadows, ivory highlights, red details, transparent holes and the complete outer edge. An alpha histogram cannot by itself certify that there is no visible halo or fringe. Record any theme-dependent loss of detail as an observed limitation.

Use review composites only as review evidence; they are not alternate originals. Do not silently flatten, crop, recolour, invert, threshold the alpha or paint over an edge to improve a preview. Any later approved derivative must have its own filename, transformation record and checksum while retaining the original PNG byte for byte. Avoid blend modes or CSS inversion as an unreviewed dark-theme conversion.

Artwork is decorative in the category-label use case, so it is not a substitute for readable text contrast. If the art becomes essential information, the consumer needs an equivalent text description. In forced-colours or an image-failure state, the visible label and native navigation must remain useful without the picture.

## Original files and provenance

The `public/artwork/category-expansion-v1/batch-01/originals/` PNGs are generated raster originals. They are not editable SVG masters. Renaming a PNG or embedding it in an SVG wrapper does not change that provenance.

Retain the exact generation request/settings that were available, the generation receipts, original dimensions and SHA-256 values with each image. Distinguish prompt intent from output observations. Do not invent unavailable model settings, seeds, historical ART04 prompts, licenses, stock-source attribution or scientific provenance.

These proposed emblems do not replace official technology logos or imply vendor endorsement. The six existing ART04 concepts and custom-v6 symbols remain preserved.

## Review and integration status

Artwork review should cover subject distinction, complete silhouette, actual crop/margins, small-size legibility, light/dark edges and the absence of readable fake text or watermarks. Keep concrete observations separate from aesthetic judgments. The batch's per-file review records and manifest report completed checks and remaining limitations.

No browser acceptance is established by this packet. Contact sheets and size/edge composites show raster appearance; they do not prove responsive page layout, keyboard/focus behavior, native navigation, forced-colour rendering or deployment. Integrating owners must inspect their actual consuming surface without changing this batch's taxonomy and ownership boundary.
