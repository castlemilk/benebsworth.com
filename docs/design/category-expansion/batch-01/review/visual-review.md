## Family and visual findings

The family is coherent in warm ivory material, graphite engraving and restrained red details. Its ten subject silhouettes are distinct: folded surface, faceted assembly, fern/seed, cutaway globe, stratified rock, wave, cloud/instrument, leaf/root system, twisted ladder and neuron. No generic glossy tiles or AI-brain substitution were observed. The illustrated subjects remain the focal point and the surrounding canvas remains open.

No readable text, numbers, formulae, labels, scale markings, watermark or vendor marks were observed in the selected artworks at the reviewed scales. The meteorology instrument face is deliberately blank apart from its needle and hub. Review-sheet labels are external QA text and are not part of the original PNGs.

No visibly cropped substantial tips or broad luminous halos were observed in the selected full-family images or the sampled 1:1 edges. Thin graphite/ivory boundary strokes are part of the engraved material. Fine neuron and root branches become low-contrast on warm ivory at small sizes; that limits their use as tiny standalone symbols.

## Transparency and measured margins

All 11 preserved originals contain a real alpha channel with fully transparent pixels. Alpha is verified numerically in `alpha-metrics.json`; it is not inferred from a black preview. Nine originals have some outer-border pixels at alpha 1/255. Mathematics r1 and Neuroscience r2 have entirely zero-alpha outer borders. None of the files has an outer-border pixel above alpha 16. These faint residues were not visible as a broad halo in the reviewed composites, but the originals must not be described as mathematically pristine cutouts.

The table measures the visible subject bounding box using alpha greater than 16, excluding very faint residues. The original common prompt requested 16% minimum margin on every side; only Earth r1 and the corrected Neuroscience r2 meet that exact numerical target. The other selected images retain 7.50–12.76% minimum substantial margins. They have no visible crop defect in this review, but future folio consumers should provide additional external spacing and use contain-style placement rather than cover cropping. ## Neuroscience correction

Revision 1 had intact branches but only 3.99% substantial clearance at the top (50 px of a 1254 px canvas), making it the clear margin outlier. Revision 2 reduces the subject footprint while preserving the branching-cell motif. Its substantial margins are left 362 px, top 326 px, right 363 px and bottom 344 px; minimum 26.00%. It has zero nonzero-alpha border pixels and no visible cropped tips in the inspected samples. The correction resolves the crowded framing.

The extra margin makes the subject visually smaller than the other artworks when every complete canvas is shown at 256 px. Preserve that air for a header/spot illustration, use at least a 256 px full-canvas slot, and avoid promoting it to a 64 px category glyph. No further generation correction is required for the observed margin or halo issue.
