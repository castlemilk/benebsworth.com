# Light and dark usage

## Placement

Use these as labelled spot or header illustrations. Prefer a full-canvas presentation of at least **128 CSS px**, and larger when the conveyor stages, configuration tabs or notebook sketches need to communicate meaning. The static 64 px review shows silhouettes surviving while fine details become indistinct. Existing navigation glyphs and official brand marks retain their separate small-scale role.

Preserve the square canvas and the complete subject. Use contain-style fitting; avoid cover crops, clipped circular badges or forced full-bleed treatment. Kubernetes and Service Mesh have usable but modest visible margins, so give their consuming layout additional outer clearance. Their minimum alpha≥128 margins are 11.32% and 10.37%; the revised Tekton and Kustomize margins are 13.72% and 17.46%. Other recommended assets range from 18.66% to 23.44%.

These margins refer to the visible subject, not a promise of perfectly zero alpha in every exterior pixel. Nine first versions contain extremely faint edge pixels with alpha 1/255. Google Cloud and the three refinements have fully transparent outer edges. Median nonzero alpha is 253/255, so the main drawn material remains visually solid.

## Supported static review surfaces

The source was composited onto warm ivory `#f6f1e7` and charcoal `#202322`, at 512 px and actual 64/128 px. No broad visible glow or halo was observed under those conditions. These are review mattes, not changes to shared site tokens and not a browser acceptance claim.

Retain the embedded graphite/ivory colours. Do not invert, recolour as a mask, add glow/drop-shadow, or multiply the art into the page. Keep source PNG alpha. A different background, image interpolation or much larger display needs its own consumer review. Use the v2 Hiking image for stone, moss, forest-green and weathered-brown identity; its green/graphite needle avoids folio-red branding.

Avoid interpreting red detail as a warning, selected state, live signal or workflow result. In this family it is a small physical material accent. Do not animate the drawn machinery or routes as if they were live data without a separately specified, truthful interaction.
