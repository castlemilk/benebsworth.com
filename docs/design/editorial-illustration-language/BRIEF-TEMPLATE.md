# Reusable editorial illustration brief

Copy this form for one article illustration. Resolve the relationship and factual limits before generating an image.

## Commission

- Article and section:
- Reader's question at this point:
- One sentence the illustration helps the reader understand:
- Visible relationship: compare / reveal / transform / relate / other:
- Fidelity class: illustrative / mathematical / data-derived / simulation:
- What must remain true:
- What the image must not imply:
- Source evidence or explicit “invented construction”:
- Relationship to existing art and why a new image is needed:

## Construction

- Dominant mass and counter-shape:
- Focal negative space or visible change:
- Viewpoint and reading direction:
- Physical material and line grammar:
- Quiet area and bounded hatch areas:
- One accent location, also distinguished by shape:
- Canvas and intrinsic margin target:
- Minimum intended size:
- Exact labels to keep outside the image:
- Light/dark and mono review plan:

## Generation or authorship

- Stable artwork ID and immutable revision:
- Exact prompt or editable source:
- Source images with role: context / geometry ancestor / edit target / none:
- Rights and licence evidence for each external input:
- Exposed tool settings:
- Unexposed model/seed/quality settings: unknown, do not infer:
- Original returned bytes, dimensions and SHA-256:
- Subsequent revisions and why each exists:

## Acceptance

- Is the focal relationship obvious at 320 px?
- Are subject and gesture still distinguishable at 160 px?
- Do solid surfaces and actual holes remain distinguishable on paper and charcoal?
- Does the silhouette carry meaning without the red accent?
- Are the intended margins present in the actual alpha channel?
- Does hatching describe a plane instead of making noise?
- Are the caption and alt text faithful to the actual result?
- Is any scientific, mathematical or mechanical claim stronger than the evidence?
- Are original and revised bytes preserved separately?
- Has the appropriate owner agreed before a shared registry/layout change?

Record retain / refine / reject with an observation. Keep assistant recommendations distinct from the user's choices.

## Prompt skeleton

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one original article-scale editorial illustration for the research folio.
Visible relationship: [state the actual relationship].
Subject and construction: [mass, counter-shape, viewpoint, continuity and focal event].
Medium: solid ivory planes, strong graphite contours, selective contour-following hatching.
Accent: one restrained red physical detail, identifiable by shape and position.
Canvas: landscape 3:2, 12-18% clear intrinsic margins around substantial artwork.
Background: true RGBA transparency, including genuine holes; no page rectangle, shadow, glow or checkerboard.
Scale: relationship legible at 320 px; texture subordinate.
Outside the image: all text, labels, equations, units, citations and annotation.
Fidelity: [explicit truth contract].
Preserve: [invariants for an edit].
Avoid: existing category art, logos, decorative symbols, invented measurement and unrelated hardware detail.

For factual diagrams, replace the image-generation route with the source-backed drawing/data workflow that can preserve exact relationships. Do not ask image generation to fabricate precision.
