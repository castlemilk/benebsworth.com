## Corrections made before freezing r1

The first Margin fold drawing suggested an external-link arrow. It was redrawn as a curled return without a diagonal. The optical sheet's size headings initially sat ambiguously above their columns; they were centered and separated from the theme labels. Both corrected outputs were re-rendered and inspected. These were bounded corrections, not additional concepts or user preferences.

## Distinctions retained in use

- **Note seat / Extent ends:** share a family resemblance, but the first is connected and the second has two separated terminals. Keep their roles distinct.
- **Paired cuts:** resembles crop or focus corners. It accompanies an explicit note label and must not become an unlabelled control or selection state.
- **Excerpt notch:** may read as a brace and equals sign. Keep it in attributed source metadata, outside code and equations.
- **Record gap:** its open form does not identify a source state by itself. The exact visible context supplies the meaning.
- **Leader landing / Extent ends:** remain annotation accessories. They do not encode dimensions, force, uncertainty, graph edges, ports or calibrated quantities.
- **Margin fold:** retains a page-turn metaphor only; no navigation, save or bookmark behavior is implied.
