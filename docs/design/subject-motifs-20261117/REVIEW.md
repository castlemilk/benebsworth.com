## Derivatives and preservation

ImageMagick created full-canvas Lanczos thumbnails and receiving-surface review composites only. Pillow decoded files and measured alpha; it did not generate, repaint or edit the artwork. There was no crop, tint, inversion, threshold, background removal, SVG trace or geometrical reconstruction. Hashes identify the exact generator-returned originals separately from every derivative.

Source verification found all fifty prior selected originals equal to their recorded SHA-256 and byte size. Packet checks decode all new PNGs, verify manifest dimensions/hashes and derivative ancestry, and constrain all proposed changes to the two owned directories.
