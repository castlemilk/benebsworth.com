# Kolibri editorial brief

Audience: engineers learning how to read a model architecture and technical report. The study follows one token, reconstructs parameter and cache budgets, then explains data, optimisation, SFT, RL and evaluation. The companion essay follows the same token through the model and ties the architecture to serving costs.

The source of empirical results is Aleph Alpha's report. Worked examples are explicitly derived or illustrative. The study notes include the source discrepancies and the conditions of the reported throughput and long-context claims.

The study is a research packet with diagrams, audio, quiz and exports. The blog is a review draft.
