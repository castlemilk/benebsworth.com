Twenty-five original constructed vector studies in five groups. All masters are editable 900 × 900 SVGs with transparent backgrounds. Each has authored light, dark and monochrome palettes.

These are illustrative fixed snapshots. Detailed bases, approximations and scientific sources follow. They are not measurements, clinical diagrams, mechanical plans or general-purpose simulation engines. No source artwork or Smithsonian specimen image is reproduced.

## 19 · Hall effect

**Basis.** For negative carriers drifting right through a magnetic field into the page, magnetic force is toward the lower strip edge.
