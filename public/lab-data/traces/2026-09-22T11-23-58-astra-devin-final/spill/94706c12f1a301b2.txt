    <h1>Solve the system over the reals</h1>
    <p class="math">x² + y² = 25, &nbsp; xy = 12.</p>
    <div class="answer">
      <p><strong>All real solution pairs (x, y):</strong></p>
      <p class="math">(4, 3), (3, 4), (−3, −4), (−4, −3).</p>
    </div>

    <h2>1. Find the sum and difference</h2>
    <p>Expanding the square of the sum and substituting the two given equations gives</p>
    <p class="math">(x + y)² = x² + y² + 2xy = 25 + 24 = 49.</p>
    <p>Since x + y is real, its square is 49 exactly when <strong>x + y = 7 or −7</strong>. Similarly,</p>
    <p class="math">(x − y)² = x² + y² − 2xy = 25 − 24 = 1,</p>
    <p>so <strong>x − y = 1 or −1</strong>. Both signs must be retained in each equation.</p>

    <h2>2. Recover x and y for every sign combination</h2>
    <p>Let s = x + y and d = x − y. Adding and subtracting these identities yields 2x = s + d and 2y = s − d. Dividing by the nonzero number 2 gives</p>
    <p class="math">x = (s + d)/2, &nbsp; y = (s − d)/2.</p>

    <h2>3. Verify the solutions and completeness</h2>
    <p>Each listed pair has squared coordinates 16 and 9, so x² + y² = 25. Its coordinates have the same sign and magnitudes 3 and 4, so xy = 12.</p>
    <p>Every real solution must have s ∈ {7, −7} and d ∈ {1, −1}. The table exhausts these four combinations, and each determines a unique pair through the formulas above. Thus all four listed pairs are solutions, and there are no others.</p>
